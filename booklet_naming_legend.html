Booklet Naming Standard

How to title a booklet assignment so the session dashboard can tell which book it is, which child it belongs to, and whether one has been missed.

[REDO|REISSUE|REASSIGN] SERIES level-booklet [HC] [FIC]

TEST: SERIES Level level

blue required grey only when it applies [ ] optional | one or the other

Series — always name one

The series tells the dashboard which curriculum a booklet belongs to. Without it, a title like 6-12 HC could be maths or English, and the dashboard has to guess.

PrefixSeriesLevelsBooklets in a level
BTMBasic Thinking Maths 1 – 241 – 18
CTMCritical Thinking Maths 1 – 2419 – 30
ENGEnglish reading A–I, then 6, 7, 81 – 30
Essay Writing:English writing A – M1 in A–E · 4 from F

Modifiers — in front of the series

A booklet appearing twice is normally a mistake, and the dashboard flags it. These two prefixes say the repeat was deliberate, so it passes quietly.

PrefixUse it whenEffect
REDO The child is redoing the booklet after a failed level test Repeat allowed
REISSUE The physical book was lost and you are replacing it Repeat allowed
REASSIGN The child did not finish it — they get another week to turn it in Repeat allowed

Markers — after the number

MarkerMeans
HCHard copy — the physical book was handed over
FICFix in class — sit with the child before the next booklet

Level tests

Naming the series matters here too: maths has a Basic and a Critical test at the same level, and Level 6 exists in both rooms.

✓TEST: BTM Level 18 Maths, basic thinking
✓TEST: CTM Level 12 Maths, critical thinking — a different test from BTM Level 12
✓TEST: ENG Level I English reading

In the test's description, record the outcome when a child does not pass: REDO: 12-19, 12-20, 12-22 lists the booklets to repeat, and ADVANCE: covered long division twice already records a decision to move on anyway. Put these in the description, not the comments — the dashboard cannot read comments.

Worked examples

✓BTM 15-17 HC Maths basic, level 15, booklet 17, physical book handed over
✓ENG I-23 HC English reading, level I, booklet 23
✓ENG H - 4 HC FIC Spacing around the hyphen is fine
✓Essay Writing: F-1 HC English writing booklet, level F, booklet 1
✓REDO BTM 12-9 HC Repeating booklet 9 after the level 12 test — series still named
✓REISSUE ENG F-12 HC Replacing a lost book
✓REASSIGN ENG G-12 HC Last week's booklet came back unfinished — another week to turn it in. Without the prefix the dashboard reads this as the book having been handed out twice.

Three mistakes that break it

✕REDO 12-9 HC The series is missing, so the booklet becomes invisible to the dashboard — worse than no prefix at all. Write REDO BTM 12-9 HC.
✕6-12 HC Level 6 exists in both maths and English, so the room cannot be told from the title. Write BTM 6-12 HC or ENG 6-12 HC.
✕F1 Essay Writing Aug Sept - 2026 The old writing-booklet style. Write Essay Writing: F-1 HC instead.

Marking a booklet as FIC

When a graded booklet comes back needing fixes, edit the assignment that already exists — add FIC to its title and move it into Classwork. Do not create a second assignment for the same booklet.

✓BTM 11-8 HC  →  BTM 11-8 HC FIC Same assignment, renamed and moved from Graded Assignments to Math Classwork. One record, so the child's history stays straight.
✕a new "BTM 11-8 FIC" alongside the graded one Two records for one booklet. The dashboard reads that as the book having been handed out twice and flags it as a duplicate.

One more thing that isn't about naming: a booklet left as a Draft in Classroom is invisible to the child, so the dashboard treats it as not handed over and will flag it. Publish it before the session ends.

Titles created before this standard are still recognised, so there is nothing to rename. This applies to new assignments only.
